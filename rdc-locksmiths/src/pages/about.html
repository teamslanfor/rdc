About RDC Locksmiths

Proudly based in North Kessock, serving Inverness and the wider Highlands community.

Who we are

Your local, experienced locksmith and door specialist

RDC Locksmiths is run by Robert from North Kessock, just across the Kessock Bridge from Inverness.

We look after locks, doors and glass for homes and businesses across the Highlands. That means getting people back in when they’re locked out, changing and upgrading locks, fixing doors that won’t close or lock properly, fitting new doors and replacing broken glass.

Because it’s a local, owner-run business, you speak to the person who’ll actually do the job, and he turns up when he says he will.
