FAQs

Common questions

I’m locked out. What should I do?{{icon:plus}}

Call us on {{site.phone}}. Don’t try to force the door or lock, as that can make the job slower and more expensive. We use non-destructive entry wherever possible.

Do you only do locks?{{icon:plus}}

No. We also repair and install doors and replace broken or misted glass, so one visit can often sort the whole problem.

Can you fix a uPVC door that won’t lock?{{icon:plus}}

Usually, yes. It’s often the mechanism, hinges or alignment rather than the lock itself. We’ll find the cause and fix it.

Which areas do you cover?{{icon:plus}}

We’re based in North Kessock and cover Inverness, the Black Isle and the wider Highlands. Not sure if we reach you? Just ask.
